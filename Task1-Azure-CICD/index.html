01 — About Me

Learning infrastructure by building it.

BSCS graduate, Karachi.

I'm Muhammad Islah Uddin, a Computer Science graduate from Muhammad Ali Jinnah University, Karachi, working toward a career in DevOps and cloud engineering. Most of what I know comes from doing it myself — containerizing applications, wiring CI/CD pipelines, standing up Kubernetes clusters on Minikube, and provisioning AWS infrastructure with Terraform until something breaks and I have to work out why.

Before that, I spent four months as an IT intern at UBL Funds, where I got comfortable with the less glamorous side of infrastructure — Windows systems, user accounts, printers, and the kind of troubleshooting that teaches you to stay calm when something suddenly stops working.

I'm currently interning with ProStackHub and recently completed a DevOps internship with Progree, both spent deepening the same stack. I'm looking for a junior DevOps or cloud engineering role where I can keep learning from people who've done this longer than I have.
